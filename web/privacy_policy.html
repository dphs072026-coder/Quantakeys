Privacy Policy for QuantaKeys

Effective Date: November 4, 2026

Application: QuantaKeys (Package: com.latenightbuilds.quantakeys)

Developer: LateNightBuilds

1. Overview

QuantaKeys is a cross-platform scientific agentic keyboard application. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how it is used, and how it is protected when you use QuantaKeys.

2. Data We Collect and Why

  • Email Address: When you choose to sign in or create an account to sync your custom keypads across devices, your email address is securely processed via Supabase Authentication.
  • Custom Keypads & Layouts: Custom keyboard layouts created by you are stored in our secure database (Supabase PostgreSQL) to enable seamless cross-device synchronization.
  • Device Identifiers (Hardware ID): A hashed permanent device identifier is used strictly for anti-tamper tracking of daily free AI calls to protect free tier usage against unauthorized bypass.
  • User Inputs for AI Queries: Text queries submitted when using the AI Hybrid Fix/Solve tool are sent to Groq Cloud / OpenAI API for real-time text polishing and complex equation solving. We do not store or use your AI query logs for training models.

3. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do NOT record, keylog, or store your passwords, credit card numbers, or private messages typed on standard keys.
  • We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal data to advertisers or third parties.

4. Third-Party Services

QuantaKeys integrates with the following trusted service providers:

  • Supabase: Used for secure user authentication and cloud database sync. (Supabase Privacy Policy)
  • Groq Cloud API: Used for ultra-fast Llama & Qwen model inference for equation solving and text polishing. (Groq Privacy Policy)
  • OpenAI API: Used for processing custom user OpenAI API key requests. (OpenAI Privacy Policy)

5. Data Security

All data transmitted between QuantaKeys and our cloud services is encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS/HTTPS protocols.

6. Account and Data Deletion Rights

You have the right to request deletion of your account and stored custom layouts at any time. You can sign out from the app settings, or request full data deletion by emailing us at support@quantakeys.edgeone.dev.
